Dalil, Azure DevOps test page

Your PAT is entered in the Dalil panel and stays in this browser. Data goes only to dev.azure.com.

How to test

  1. Create a personal access token at https://dev.azure.com/{your-org}/_usersSettings/tokens with only the Work Items (Read & write) scope and an expiry of 30 days or less.
  2. Fill in the settings below and press Save.
  3. Press Save booking (a request that fails) and Show error toast, and click around.
  4. Press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+B (or the floating button), paste your PAT in the Connect panel, type a title and send.
  5. Open the Bug in Azure DevOps: inline screenshots, attachments and replay-*.html (download it and open it offline).
  6. Try a wrong or expired PAT to see the error panel and Download evidence (.zip).

Settings

Basic-process projects have no Repro Steps field; set bodyField to System.Description in your own app config. This page uses the default.
